## Product Principles

1. **Credibility through proof**: Every claim backed by metrics, case studies, or demonstrated work. Numbers build trust with founder-level decision-makers.

2. **Design is the product**: The portfolio itself must exemplify the design quality and technical excellence Randy delivers for clients. Slow or inaccessible site contradicts the value proposition.

3. **Clarity over cleverness**: Founders evaluating services need to understand value proposition, approach, and proof points immediately. No mystery meat navigation or obscured CTAs.

4. **Performance signals competence**: Fast load times, flawless mobile experience, and accessibility compliance demonstrate the standards Randy brings to client work.

5. **AI-forward positioning**: Emphasize AI product design expertise as key differentiator in crowded fractional CDO market. This is specialized capability, not generic design leadership.
