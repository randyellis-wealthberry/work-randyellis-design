<objective>
Verify — and remediate only where genuinely missing — that the withdrawn metric-removal premise
is corrected everywhere `.planning/` records it, so it cannot regenerate a fourth time.

Purpose: this milestone's own target failure is a summary outliving its source. The
2026-08-15 deck-gate reversal was recorded in `DECK-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md`, and every downstream
summary kept the pre-reversal framing — which is how v3.0 got staged around removing three
accurate figures. REC-01 exists to close that loop.

**Ordering (D-10, and D-02).** This plan is `depends_on: ["11-01"]`, so it runs strictly after
the OG fix rather than beside it. There is no `files_modified` overlap between the two — the
dependency is declared to enforce decided ordering, and it buys two things. It satisfies D-10's
literal "sequence REC-01 **second**, right after the OG fix". And it makes D-02's "**first**"
deterministic: D-02 asks CRED-13 to ship as its own independently-shippable commit *first*, and
two plans sitting in the same wave with `depends_on: []` are parallel-eligible, so their commit
order would be arbitrary. Landing in wave 2 of 4 — concurrently with PRF-01, which is precisely
"while the rest of the phase is in flight" — costs REC-01 nothing against its stated rationale
of stopping the premise propagating early.

**This work was already applied during the `/gsd:discuss-phase` session (D-10).** This plan
VERIFIES it rather than redoing it. Task 1 audits against a concrete, grep-able checklist and
records a pass/fail per criterion. Task 2 remediates ONLY the criteria that fail — and is a
legitimate no-op if all pass. Do not rewrite text that already satisfies a criterion.
